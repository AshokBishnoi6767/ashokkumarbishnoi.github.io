About

I make complex technology easy to understand and hard to ignore.

I’m a B2B SaaS content writer and content strategist with 7+ years of experience turning complex products, technologies, and ideas into content that people actually want to read. My background in computer science gives me the technical foundation to understand what I’m writing about. My experience in SaaS, AI, data, cybersecurity, and technology helps me understand why it matters.

And years of writing have taught me how to turn all of that into clear, compelling content.

Over the years, I’ve worked across 600+ content projects for 80+ clients across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and India, from SaaS websites and technical blogs to case studies, whitepapers, sales collateral, email campaigns, and thought leadership.

One of my favorite wins? Helping a B2B SaaS company increase its monthly website traffic by 273%, from 2.6K to 7.1K users.

I don't believe good content is about using bigger words or publishing more articles. It’s about understanding the product, the audience, and the business well enough to say the right thing in the simplest possible way.

That’s what I do.
